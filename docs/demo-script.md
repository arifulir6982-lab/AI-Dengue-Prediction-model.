# 3-minute judge walkthrough

1. Launch the landing page and open the dashboard.
2. Point out the Synthetic Demo Data banner and explain that no live DGHS feed is claimed.
3. Open Historical Analytics and inspect the seasonal case/weather pattern.
4. Open Scenario Simulator. Use Chattogram, 4 weeks, rainfall 300 mm, temperature 30°C, humidity 85%; run analysis.
5. Explain the estimate as a deterministic demonstration heuristic, its baseline comparison and illustrative uncertainty—not a validated case forecast.
6. Open Bangladesh Risk Map, select a marker and explain that points are approximate city coordinates with synthetic risk indices.
7. Open Data & Model to show documented sources, planned integrations, and the no-trained-model status.
8. Finish with prevention recommendations and the health notice.
